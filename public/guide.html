JeepLink Guide

A simple guide to navigating jeepney routes in Dasmariñas City

1

Set Your Starting Point

Open the Map tab and pin your current location. This tells the app where exactly you will start your commute so we can find the nearest jeepney stops.

2

Enter Your Desired Destination

Search or pin the location where you want to go. Once all the pins are set, JeepLink will begin calculating the best possible ways to get there.

3

Compare Your Options

Review the list of available routes. For every journey, the route, fare, and jeepneys will be displayed — so you'll know which jeepney lines to take and can estimate how much it will cost.

4

Choose and Travel

Select the path that fits your needs and follow the step-by-step directions displayed based on what matters most to you:

  • Fastest — the fastest way to arrive
  • Cheapest — the most budget-friendly fare
  • Easiest — the route with the fewest transfers or walking

Commuter Tip

STAY ALERT: Always make sure to check the "Sign Board" on the side and front of the jeepney to make sure that it matches the route name displayed in the app!
