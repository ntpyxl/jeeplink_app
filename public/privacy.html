Privacy Policy

JeepLink is committed to protecting your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

At JeepLink we believe that your journey should be fluid while prioritizing your data privacy. This policy outlines how we take care of your information with total transparency.

1. Real-Time Location Access

To help commuters like you find the right ride, JeepLink requests access to your real-time location data. This will be the only personal data we use to power the app's core features.

2. How Will the Data Be Used

The location gathered will only be used for two functional purposes:

  • Discovery: To find and display the jeepney routes closest to your pinned location.
  • Navigation: To give accurate route suggestions and estimated travel time for commuters.

3. Regarding Data Storage

We made sure that privacy is our top priority and it's built into our core code.

  • No History: We do not save your location history or movement patterns in our servers.
  • Live Processing: Data gathered is processed in real time and immediately discarded after it serves its purpose or when closing JeepLink.

4. No Sharing or Selling

We are in the business of transportation, not data brokerage.

  • We will never share your location with any third parties.
  • Your data will never be sold or distributed for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security & Consent

  • Security: We employ industry-standard encryption to protect your data while it is being processed.
  • Consent: By using JeepLink's services, you agree to the real-time use of your location data as described. You can revoke this permission at any time via your device's location settings.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect new features or security standards. We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed.
