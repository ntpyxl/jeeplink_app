Privacy Policy

JeepLink is committed to protecting your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

At JeepLink, we believe your journey should be smooth while keeping your data privacy protected. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information with transparency and responsibility.

1. Real-Time Location Access

To help commuters find the right routes, JeepLink requests access to your real-time location. This is the only personal data used to power the core features of the system.

2. How Will the Data Be Used

The location data collected is used only for the following purposes:

  • Discovery: To show jeepney routes closest to your current location.
  • Navigation: To provide route suggestions and estimated travel time.

3. Incident Reporting Feature

When users submit an incident report (such as road issues, accidents, or delays):

  • The system may temporarily capture the current location at the time of reporting to properly tag the incident.
  • This location data is stored without identifying the user.
  • No personal identity, device tracking, or travel history is linked to the report.
  • The data is used only to display and document reported incidents for system awareness and route improvement.

4. Data Storage Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your data. Here's how we handle the storage of your location information:

  • No Location History: We do not store your movement or travel history.
  • Live Processing: Location data is processed in real time and discarded immediately after use.
  • Anonymous Incident Data: Incident reports store only the reported location without user identification.

5. No Sharing or Selling

  • We do not share your location with third parties.
  • We do not sell or use your data for marketing purposes.

6. Data Security & Consent

We implement robust security measures to protect your data. By using JeepLink's services, you agree to the real-time use of your location data as described. You can revoke this permission at any time via your device's location settings.

  • Security: We use industry-standard security measures and encryption to protect data during processing.
  • Consent: By using JeepLink, you agree to the real-time use of your location data as described. You may revoke location access anytime through your device settings.

7. Compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012

JeepLink complies with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines, ensuring that all collected data is handled lawfully, fairly, and transparently. We uphold your rights as a data subject, including the right to access, correct, and object to data processing as applicable.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
